LESSON 1

What Is Digital Marketing?

Digital marketing means promoting a product, service, or brand using the internet and digital channels.

Where can digital marketing happen?

  • Social media, such as Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
  • Email newsletters and messages.
  • Websites, blogs, and search engines.
  • Online advertisements and videos.

Example

A small bakery posts cake pictures on Instagram, explains its prices, and tells customers how to order. This is an example of digital marketing.

Remember

Good marketing starts by understanding the people you want to reach and what they need.
