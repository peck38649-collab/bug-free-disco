What Is Graphic Design?

Graphic design is the art of using pictures, colours, text and layouts to communicate a message.

Examples of graphic design

  • Social media flyers
  • Business logos
  • Posters and banners
  • Product advertisements

Four important design elements

1. Colour: Creates a feeling and attracts attention.

2. Typography: The style and arrangement of text.

3. Images: Help people understand your message.

4. Layout: How you arrange everything on a design.
