Lesson 1: What Is Content Creation?

Content creation means making useful or interesting material to share with an audience. It can include videos, pictures, written posts, stories, and audio.

Examples of content

  • A short video explaining something.
  • A picture with an informative caption.
  • A social media post teaching a useful tip.
  • A short story that entertains people.

How to create good content

  1. Choose the audience you want to reach.
  2. Pick a topic they may find useful or interesting.
  3. Decide whether to make a video, picture, or written post.
  4. Create your content with a clear message.
  5. Share it and learn from feedback.

🎬 Practical Lesson 1: Plan Your First Video

Welcome to Content Creation! In this lesson, you will plan a short video before recording it.

Step 1: Choose your video topic

Pick something you know or want to teach. For example: “3 ways to stay productive” or “How to organise your phone.”

Step 2: Know your audience

Ask yourself: Who will watch my video, and what will they learn from it?

Step 3: Write a simple script

Use this easy structure:

  • Hook: Get attention with a question or interesting statement.
  • Value: Share 2 or 3 helpful points.
  • Ending: Tell viewers what to do next.

Example script

Hook: “Do you want to make better use of your phone?”

Value: “Organise your apps, remove distractions, and use your notes app to plan your day.”

Ending: “Follow for more simple digital tips!”
